Place the official Lenovo logo files here before deploying:

  lenovo-logo.svg        - full-color logo, used on light backgrounds
  lenovo-logo-light.svg  - white/reversed logo, used on the dark navy header

PNG works too (lenovo-logo.png / lenovo-logo-light.png) if you update the
`src` paths in components/Logo.tsx to match. No fabricated Lenovo logo is
included in this codebase - only your own official brand asset files should
go here.
